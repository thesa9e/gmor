# Generative Model of Reality

**English** | [Русский](README.ru.md)

This is the public repository for an original generative model of how reality
unfolds and folds back into potential.

[The current account of the model and related materials in English](https://thesa9e.com)
are published on the project website.

The model explores a general principle by which reality unfolds from a potential
state through constraints and actualization, forms stable structures, and may
fold back into a more potential state.

## Repository and website

This repository is intended as a public body of self-contained, versioned
artifacts of the model. It will include:

- diagrams and formal models;
- original hypotheses;
- definitions, ontological schemas, and glossaries;
- selected versions of textual accounts and interpretations;
- other materials whose history of change is of interest in its own right.

Each artifact may have a different status: draft, working hypothesis, stable
version, or archive.

A coherent account of the model, its current interpretation, and the context
needed to approach it are published on the [project website](https://thesa9e.com).
The author's internal working materials are kept separately.

## How to read the model

The diagram is the primary artifact. Before interpreting it, use the official
authorial reading frame appropriate to the reader:

- [reading frame for a human reader](guides/reality-unfolding.human.en.md);
- [interpretation frame for a generative model](guides/reality-unfolding.ai.en.md).

The frames define how to read the diagram without silently importing concepts and
relations that it has not yet made available. They constrain the process of
interpretation, not its conclusion, and are not additional ontological claims.

The complete list of published materials, their versions, status, language, and
purpose is maintained in the [artifact index](ARTIFACTS.md).

## Status

The model is being made public gradually.

Published materials describe an original working model. They are not presented
as an established scientific theory unless a specific document explicitly states
otherwise.

The official sources for the project are:

- this repository;
- the [project website](https://thesa9e.com);
- announcements made by the author through the project's official channels.

Forks, copies, and derivative materials are not official versions of the model
unless the author explicitly identifies them as such.
